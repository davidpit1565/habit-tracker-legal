When you write, include your iOS version, the app version and a description of the problem. To find the app version, open the app, go to Settings, then About. Please do not send private habit notes or backup files unless needed.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and no online sync, so habits do not appear on other devices.

How do I back up or restore my habits?

Open Settings and choose Export backup. Save the file somewhere you control. To restore, choose Import backup. Importing replaces all habits in the app, including archived ones, after you confirm. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it safe.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Delete all local data, then confirm. This removes your habits and history. It does not remove backup files you exported earlier, and it does not reset your appearance setting.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check that notifications are allowed for Habit Tracker in iOS Settings, that the habit is not archived, and that it has a reminder time. Focus modes and other iOS settings can also hide notifications.

How do I restore Pro, or what does Pro include?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID that bought Pro. Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription ($5.99 per month or $49.99 per year). You can cancel any time in your App Store account settings. It adds unlimited habits, time goals, Apple Reminders sync, checking habits off from the widget, habit colors, full-year history and statistics, CSV export, alternate app icons and more streak protection. People who installed the app before Pro was introduced keep every feature free. For billing questions, use Apple's Report a Problem page, because Apple handles all payments.

Does the app work on iPad or sync between devices?

Habit Tracker is built for iPhone and has no sync. Use Export and Import backup to move habits to another iPhone.
